Why I built this

Hi, I'm Susie. I made MoodMove because I love training, but I almost never want to do the same thing two days in a row. Some days I'm ready to lift heavy. Other days I'd rather run on the treadmill, do a Hyrox workout, or swim. Most workout apps hand you one rigid plan, and on the days that plan doesn't match how I feel, I end up skipping the session altogether.

MoodMove starts each day with a suggested workout — but if you're not feeling it, you just swipe. Like a dating app for training, you browse other routines and match with the one that fits your energy, your mood, and your goals for that day. For me, having options is exactly what keeps me consistent, and consistency is what actually gets results.

The app keeps a calendar of every session you complete, so your progress is easy to look back on, and every user keeps their own notes on each workout — how it felt, the weights they used, what to try next time. The goal is simple: make training feel less like a rule you have to follow and more like a choice you get to make.
